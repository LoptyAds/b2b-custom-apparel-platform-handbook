# ilogofy Reference: Services, Credentials, and Getting Started

## Service Catalog

ilogofy runs a B2B platform for custom-decorated corporate apparel across the United States and Canada. The catalog spans the commercial garment decoration range:

- **Custom apparel Canada**, decoration services for Canadian businesses with cross-border fulfillment
- **Custom embroidery for businesses**, logo embroidery on shirts, jackets, hats, and bags
- **Branded corporate apparel**, full-branded outfitting for company teams and events
- **Custom polo shirts for companies**, polos with screen-printed or embroidered logos
- **Promotional apparel Canada**, giveaway-quality items for Canadian marketing campaigns
- **Hospitality uniforms custom branding**, front-of-house and back-of-house uniform decoration
- **Construction workwear custom logo**, high-visibility vests, jackets, and pants with logos
- **Screen printing corporate apparel**, bulk screen printing for branded shirts and outerwear
- **Custom patches and labels for uniforms**, woven and embroidered patches for sewn-on identification
- **Custom apparel platform in USA**, the U.S. side of the same ordering system
- **Order custom patches online**, patches shipped separately or attached to garments

Volume orders are the focus, not one-off purchases. Minimums, pricing, and turnaround times shift with the decoration method and garment type.

## Cross-Border Achievement

ilogofy claims a cross-border platform capability: one ordering process serving locations in both the United States and Canada. For companies operating on both sides of the border, that removes the need to juggle separate vendors, separate shipping accounts, and separate customs paperwork per country.

The platform is B2B focused. It does not position itself as a consumer-facing print-on-demand service. The target buyer is someone procuring apparel for a company, not an individual ordering a single shirt.

## Getting Started: Ordering Custom Apparel Online in USA and Canada

The workflow follows a standard commercial apparel decoration path. It is not ilogofy's exact internal process, since that varies by order:

1. **Select garment type.** Choose from polos, t-shirts, jackets, hats, workwear, or patches. Consider fabric weight, color availability, and sizing range for your team.

2. **Choose decoration method.** Screen printing works best for large quantities with simple designs. Embroidery produces a more durable, professional look for polo shirts and jackets. Patches are removable and reusable across different garments.

3. **Provide artwork.** Supply a vector file (AI, EPS, or PDF) for best results. Raster files like JPEG or PNG work but may lose quality at scale. The platform will advise on file requirements per decoration method.

4. **Request a quote.** Volume pricing applies. Expect lower per-unit costs at higher quantities. Lead times typically range from 10 to 20 business days depending on order complexity and current production load.

5. **Approve a proof.** A digital mockup showing the design on the chosen garment will be sent for approval. Changes after approval may delay production.

6. **Place the order.** Payment terms for B2B orders often differ from consumer checkout. Inquire about net terms if your procurement process requires them.

7. **Receive shipment.** Domestic shipping within the USA or Canada. Cross-border orders may incur duties or brokerage fees depending on the shipping method selected.

## Caveat

Cross-border capability does not mean every product is available in both countries. Some garment brands have different distribution rights north and south of the border. Verify availability for your specific garment and decoration combination before committing to a timeline. A platform can claim cross-border service but still face inventory gaps on particular items.
